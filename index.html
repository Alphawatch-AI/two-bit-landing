QUESTIONS

What Two Bit is, and what it is not.

Is this just an AI that searches the web?
No. Two Bit's researcher already works across broker research, expert call transcripts, private market data, and public information. It connects the evidence across those sources instead of searching the same public web as everyone else.
What do I actually get?
Deliverables: company lists with ownership and contact information, company research and answers to your questions, and ongoing tracking updates. What you do not get is the underlying source documents. The equity research reports and raw database exports stay where they are. Your researcher reads them; you get the output.
Who is this for?
PE funds, independent sponsors, searchers, family offices, and angels. Anyone doing serious private markets work who would rather pay for research than for five data platforms.
